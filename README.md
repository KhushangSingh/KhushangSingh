## About Me

I'm a full-stack developer and UI/UX designer who enjoys building complete products end-to-end — from wireframes and design systems in Figma to shipping full-stack applications with the MERN stack. My approach treats wireframes as an architectural blueprint: the structure and user flow come first, and a scalable design system is what turns that blueprint into a cohesive, polished product.

Alongside my coursework, I currently serve as **Vice-President of the GeeksforGeeks Student Chapter** and **Design Lead for the Electric Vehicle Club** at VIT Bhopal, and I use industry job simulations to keep learning outside the classroom.

**McDonald's App — Heuristic Evaluation & Redesign**

- Redesigned the core navigation with a persistent sidebar, resolving a "disappearing menu" issue and reducing scroll fatigue
- Reworked the food details modal from a restrictive square layout to a wide rectangle for easier scanning of nutritional info
