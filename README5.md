## About Me

I'm a full-stack developer and UI/UX designer who likes owning a product end-to-end — from wireframes and design systems in Figma to shipping production-ready applications with the MERN stack. My approach starts with a design system rather than one-off screens, which keeps every interface consistent, scalable, and fast to iterate on.

Alongside my coursework at VIT Bhopal, I lead design and community initiatives across three student clubs, and I use hackathons and industry job simulations to keep learning outside the classroom.

- 🎓 B.Tech, Computer Science Engineering — Vellore Institute of Technology, Bhopal (CGPA 8.71/10)
- 🎯 Vice-President, GeeksforGeeks Student Chapter · Design Lead, The Electric Vehicle Club
- 💡 Focused on full-stack product development, UI/UX systems, and cloud fundamentals (AWS)

## 🎯 UX Case Study

**McDonald's App — Heuristic Evaluation & Redesign**
- Conducted a heuristic evaluation of the live McDelivery platform, identifying 7 critical usability issues, including navigational friction and visual asymmetry
- Redesigned the core navigation with a persistent sidebar, resolving a "disappearing menu" issue and reducing scroll fatigue
- Reworked the food details modal from a cramped square layout to a wide rectangle for easier scanning of nutritional info
- Standardized spacing and cart preview margins for a more polished visual hierarchy
